INTERPRETATION
====================================================================================================

GNN Approach:
  - Learns temporal patterns at the TOPIC level
  - Each topic is a semantic cluster assigned to papers
  - Predictions indicate which broad research areas are trending
  - Keywords are secondary: identified post-hoc to explain topic predictions

Itemset Mining Approach:
  - Discovers KEYWORD COMBINATIONS that frequently co-occur
  - Uses residual-based forecasting to identify unexpected growth
  - Predictions are keyword-centric: specific research sub-areas
  - Can capture fine-grained research patterns (e.g., 'llm + reasoning')

Complementarity:
  - GNN gives broad research trend (e.g., 'Deep Learning')
  - Itemset shows what's driving it (e.g., 'reasoning + reinforcement')
  - Together: get both macro trends AND micro research directions
